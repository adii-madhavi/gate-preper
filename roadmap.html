Roadmap to February 2027

A week-by-week plan from November 2026 to the end of the exam window. Concept weeks list every official syllabus topic as its own task, with a lecture video and past questions. It assumes you have seen most subjects once. If you are starting from zero, cover the higher-weight subjects first (programming and data structures, COA, OS, CN, TOC, maths) and keep the rest for revision. Tick tasks as you finish them; progress is saved only in this browser.
