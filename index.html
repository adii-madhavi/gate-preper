Your CS paper date and session will be announced by the organisers; 6 Feb 2027 is only the first day of the exam window. Dates, syllabus and pattern can change. Always check the official GATE 2027 website (IIT Madras), including its question paper pattern and revised CS syllabus (PDF).

About the marks figures: GATE does not fix marks per CS subject. Ranges below are historical marks per paper from the GO Classes analysis of the 2023–2025 GATE CSE papers; 2025 figures are that analysis’s average of the two 2025 sessions. They describe past papers and do not predict 2027. That analysis reports marks, not question counts, so question counts are not shown per subject.

Roadmap to February 2027

A week-by-week plan from November 2026 to the end of the exam window. It assumes you have seen most subjects once. If you are starting from zero, cover the higher-weight subjects first (programming and data structures, COA, OS, CN, TOC, maths) and keep the rest for revision. Tick tasks as you finish them; progress is saved only in this browser.
